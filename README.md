# Online Food Ordering App — Scrum Project

This repository organizes the Online Food Ordering App assignment using GitHub Issues and GitHub Projects.

## Project goal
Plan an online food ordering experience where customers can sign in, browse restaurants and menus, manage a cart, pay online, and track delivery.

## Product backlog
| ID | User story | Priority | Story points | Planned sprint |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| US01 | Customer Login | High | 3 | Sprint 1 |
| US02 | Browse Restaurants and Menu | High | 5 | Sprint 1 |
| US03 | Shopping Cart | High | 5 | Sprint 1 |
| US04 | Online Payment | High | 8 | Sprint 2 |
| US05 | Order Tracking | Medium | 5 | Sprint 2 |

Story points are relative complexity/effort estimates, not hours.

## Sprint 1
**Goal:** Allow customers to log in, browse the menu, and add food items to the cart.

Selected stories: US01, US02, US03.

## Sprint 2
Selected stories: US04 Online Payment and US05 Order Tracking.
